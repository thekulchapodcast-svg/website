03 / The story

"We follow the culture. We endorse the culture. We create the culture."

The Kulcha is a community-based movement from Pretoria — a podcast, an events collective, a talent platform, and a sound equipment hire service, all built around the same thing: the music, the culture, and everything in between.

We cover the scene other outlets skip: underground artists, cannabis culture, and the events that actually move the community forward.
