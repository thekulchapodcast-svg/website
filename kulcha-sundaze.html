01 / What it is

"Sundaze isn't a rave. It's a reset."

Kulcha Sundaze is our weekly gathering — a chilled but vibrant Sunday session where live sets, DJs, food, and the community come together. It's where the week winds down and the culture winds up.

Whether you're pulling up for the music, the people, or just the vibe, Sundaze is built to feel like home every time.
